WonderPlay

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 18, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to WonderPlay. WonderPlay is an educational game designed to provide an interactive and enjoyable learning experience for primary school children.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the WonderPlay application.

We respect your privacy and have designed the current version of WonderPlay to operate without requiring an online account or transmitting the player's profile information to our servers.

2. Information You Enter in the App

WonderPlay currently provides a Player page where a player can enter information such as:

  • Player name
  • Player age
This information is currently stored only on the player's device using local storage. WonderPlay does not transmit this information to a WonderPlay server.

The player name and age are used only to provide the player experience within the application.

3. Information We Collect

The current version of WonderPlay does not collect personal information from the device for transmission to us.

In particular, the current version does not collect or transmit:

  • Player names
  • Player ages
  • Email addresses
  • Phone numbers
  • Precise location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or videos
  • Financial or payment information
  • Authentication information

WonderPlay also does not currently require users to create an account.

4. Local Storage

WonderPlay uses local storage on the device to remember certain player information and game-related preferences.

Information stored locally remains on the user's device and is not transmitted to WonderPlay.

If the WonderPlay application is uninstalled, locally stored application data may be removed by the operating system.

5. Advertising

The current version of WonderPlay does not display advertisements and does not contain an advertising SDK.

WonderPlay therefore does not currently use advertising identifiers for advertising purposes.

6. Analytics and Tracking

The current version of WonderPlay does not include an analytics SDK or third-party tracking technology.

We therefore do not currently use third-party analytics services to track individual players or their activity within the app.

7. Sharing of Information

The current version of WonderPlay does not transmit the player's locally stored profile information to WonderPlay or third parties.

Accordingly, we do not currently sell, rent, or share player profile information with third parties.

8. Children's Privacy

WonderPlay is designed for primary school children and is intended to provide an age-appropriate educational gaming experience.

The current version of WonderPlay does not require children to create an online account or provide contact information.

The player name and age entered in the app are stored locally on the device and are not transmitted to WonderPlay servers.

Parents or guardians who have questions about the information handled by WonderPlay are welcome to contact us using the details provided below.

9. Data Security

Because the current version of WonderPlay does not transmit player profile information to our servers, we do not maintain a server-side database of player names or ages.

Users should keep their device secure and use the security features provided by their device operating system.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Player information stored by WonderPlay is stored locally on the device.

Users can remove locally stored WonderPlay data by uninstalling the application.

Since the current version does not transmit player profile information to our servers, WonderPlay does not maintain a server-side copy of the player's name or age.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when WonderPlay's features, services, or data practices change.

When changes are made, the updated Privacy Policy will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

If future versions of WonderPlay introduce features such as analytics, advertising, online accounts, cloud services, or other data processing, this Privacy Policy will be updated to describe those practices as applicable.
